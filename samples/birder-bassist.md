# Taste dossier (fictional sample persona)

## Obsessions
- Birdwatching at dawn; keeps a life list and is two warblers away from 300 species
- Plays bass in a garage band that has had four names this year
- Victorian lighthouses and their Fresnel lenses

## Questions I keep asking
- Coffee brew ratios and water temperature (about 20 conversations)
- How to identify birds by song, especially "is that a wren or a car alarm?" (about 10)
- Music theory for walking bass lines (about 8)

## Things I love
- Field notebooks, brass instruments, foghorns
- Cozy mystery novels set on islands
- Synthesizers from the 1980s

## Artists and styles
- Katsushika Hokusai: has a print of The Great Wave and asked about his bird studies
- Victorian botanical and ornithological engravings

## Quirks
- Rates every coffee out of ten like a bird sighting ("a solid seven, possibly a rare roast")
- Names band songs after birds they haven't seen yet
- Hums bass lines while waiting for herons
